Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

This is Jan Ackermann's personal website. For privacy questions or requests, please use the contact address on the homepage.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. These providers process technical request data, such as IP addresses and requested pages, to operate and protect the site. I use Cloudflare's server-side statistics to understand overall traffic. Their policies explain their data handling: GitHub and Cloudflare.

The site has no accounts or forms, and no advertising trackers or browser analytics scripts are added. Fonts and icons are hosted locally. Cloudflare may use security cookies to protect the site.

Your light or dark mode choice is saved in your browser. It stays there until you change it or clear this site's browser data.
